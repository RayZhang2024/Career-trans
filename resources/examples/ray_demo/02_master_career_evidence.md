# Evidence Classification Rules

Career Agent should classify candidate evidence into four levels:

### Demonstrated
Direct evidence of having performed the capability.

### Transferable
Strong adjacent evidence indicates likely ability, but the candidate has not demonstrated the exact context.

### Inferred
Plausible based on background but insufficient for a strong claim.

### Missing
No supporting evidence in the resource set.

The agent must not convert an inferred capability into a demonstrated capability.
